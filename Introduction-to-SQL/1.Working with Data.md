### Understanding Database Tables

Most databases collect data in tables. Tables organize data into **records** (rows) and **fields** (columns).

- **Rows** represent **records**.
- **Columns** represent **fields**.

A **record** (row) contains information about a subject such as an employee, customer, or product (like a movie).

A **field** (column) contains information about the subjects, such as name, email, or release year.

The **header row** is the top row of a table, containing the names for different fields (columns).
